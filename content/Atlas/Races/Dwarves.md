- Dwarves are the shepherds of this [[Iterum]]'s [[Reality Engine]]
- Generally revered as mythological entities that generally in a positive light

# **Rumors of Lore**

1. **The Silent Architects** – Legends speak of vast underground vaults where the dwarves left behind impossible machines, humming with power but impossible to activate. Some say these vaults contain the last remnants of their kind, frozen in time, waiting for the right moment to return.
2. **The World That Almost Was** – A whispered tale among scholars claims that the dwarves tried to build an artificial paradise, a perfect world within the **Reality Engine**, but it collapsed due to their hubris, nearly unmaking the Iterum itself.
3. **The Great Seal** – Old stories claim the dwarves built a final, unknowable barrier between this world and the chaos beyond. It is said that if the seal were ever broken, the world would dissolve like mist at dawn.
4. **The Last Bargain** – Some believe the dwarves struck a desperate deal with an entity beyond the veil—one that required them to erase their own history in exchange for keeping the Iterum stable. This is why so little remains of their true past.
5. **The Unfinished Machine** – It is said that somewhere, buried beneath the ruins of the old world, lies the Reality Engine’s final piece—something the dwarves never completed. If found, it could either stabilize the Iterum forever or unravel it completely.
6. **The Waking of the Watchers** – Old seers claim that one day, when the stars align, the dwarves will return from beyond the veil, bringing with them knowledge to either save or destroy the world.
7. **The Song of the Deep Places** – A strange, rhythmic hum can be heard in certain caverns and ruins at night. Some say it is the dwarves’ last song, still resonating across time, guiding those who might one day take up their burden.


# **Truths**

1. **The Eternal Vigil** – The dwarves did not perish; they retreated to a hidden plane—a liminal space between Iterums—where they fight an endless battle against an entity that could consume this reality if they falter.
2. **The Fractured Oath** – Once, the dwarves were stewards of all Iterums, but something went terribly wrong. Now, they are trapped, able to influence only this one, using their last remaining strength to prevent its collapse.
3. **The Machine and the Maker** – The Reality Engine was not just built—it was **grown**, shaped over countless ages by dwarven artificers. But something within it rebelled, a force that refuses to be controlled. The dwarves’ self-imposed exile is as much about containing it as it is about protecting humanity.
4. **The Prison That Became a Home** – What was meant to be a temporary containment for the entity beyond the Iterum became a purgatory for the dwarves themselves. They can never return, lest they risk bringing it with them.
5. **The Silent Hand** – While most assume the dwarves are gone, echoes of their influence remain. Certain artifacts, locations, and even bloodlines are still connected to them, occasionally shifting reality in subtle ways to preserve the Iterum.
6. **The Legacy Shattered** – The dwarves were once part of the **Legacy**, but their actions splintered them from it. Some ancient records indicate that the other factions of the Legacy might have betrayed them, forcing them into their exile.
7. **The Signal** – Deep within the world's ruins, there is a buried structure still transmitting a dwarven distress signal. It is unknown whether it is meant for this world—or something else.
