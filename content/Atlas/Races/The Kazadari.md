# Overview

- Any creature that has notable features related to dwarven heritage as list below
- The name means: "Those of the Mountain’s Shadow" in [[The Old Tongue]]

# Possible Notable Features

### **Physical Features**

1. **Geomorphic Growth** – Their bodies subtly mimic **rock formations**, with veins of metal, crystalline growths, or rough, stone-like skin.
2. **Braided Flesh** – Some Kazadari exhibit **natural braiding patterns in their hair, fur, or even musculature**, as if their very being follows dwarven artistry.
3. **Stonemarrow Bones** – Their skeletons are **denser, almost stone-like**, making them far heavier than they appear.
4. **Echoing Voice** – Their voices resonate **as if spoken in a cavern**, deep and layered, even when whispering.
5. **Ironclad Fingertips** – Fingernails and claws resemble **wrought iron or dulled metal**, unbreakable and sometimes sparking against stone.
6. **Forge-Touched Skin** – Their bodies retain heat **longer than normal**, as if their ancestors were shaped near molten metal.
7. **Gemstone Eyes** – Their irises may be **faceted like crystals**, glinting unnaturally in dim light.
8. **Anvil-Heart** – Their heartbeat is **slow and rhythmic**, almost mechanical, and **barely detectable** unless under extreme exertion.
9. **Veins of Ore** – Some Kazadari have **traces of actual metal running through their veins**, visible beneath their skin like shimmering threads.
10. **Stone-Eater’s Teeth** – Their molars are **flat and ridged**, built for grinding tougher materials, sometimes allowing them to chew raw minerals for sustenance.

### **Behavioral & Sensory Traits**

11. **Tremorsense** – A subtle awareness of vibrations in the ground, allowing them to **"hear" footsteps or movement without sight**.
12. **Stone’s Patience** – They **age more slowly** than normal for their kind, moving through life with an **unnatural endurance and methodical nature**.
13. **Resistance to Time’s Wear** – Their bodies **resist decay and erosion**, making them appear **untouched by time or wounds that should scar**.
14. **Forgesoul** – When angered or in battle, their body **radiates heat**, and in rare cases, faint embers rise from their breath.
15. **Unyielding Stance** – They are incredibly difficult to **push, pull, or knock down**, as if they were naturally anchored to the earth.
16. **The Call of Deep Places** – A subconscious **pull toward caverns, mountains, or ruins**, as if something forgotten still calls to them.
17. **Hammerborn Instinct** – Even without training, they can intuitively **use a hammer, chisel, or forge tools** with unnatural precision.
18. **Earthbound Loyalty** – A **deep, instinctual connection to oaths and contracts**, often feeling physical pain or unease if forced to break their word.
19. **Hearthbound Will** – They sleep more soundly near **stone, metal, or fire**, often refusing to rest on anything too soft.
20. **Echo of the Past** – When idle, they may **hum forgotten dwarven songs**, tap **rhythms of ancient forges**, or absentmindedly trace lost runes.
